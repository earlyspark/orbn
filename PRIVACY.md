# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 2026_

orbn is a personal, open-source music player that runs **entirely on your own Android device.**
This policy explains what data orbn touches and where it goes. In plain terms: **your data stays
on your device, and the developer never receives any of it.**

## Who this applies to

orbn is open-source software. "You" means whoever installs and runs orbn on their own device. The
developer ([RayAna / @earlyspark](https://github.com/earlyspark)) operates **no servers** and
receives **no data** from your copy of orbn.

## What data orbn accesses

- **Your music files** on your device — to analyze them (tempo, key, energy, mood, genre) and play them.
- **Your own Oura Ring data** — only if you choose to connect your Oura account. With your
  authorization, orbn reads your readiness, sleep and HRV, heart rate, and daily stres from the Oura API, on your behalf.
- **Listening activity that orbn creates locally** — play history, skips, your manual mood/energy
  choices, and the biometric state at the time — used to personalize what it plays.

## Where your data goes

- **Nowhere off your device.** Analysis, storage, matching, and visuals all happen locally.
- orbn communicates with exactly **one** external service — the **Oura API** — and only to fetch
  *your own* data using *your* authorization. It sends nothing about you to the developer or any
  third party.
- There is **no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no account, and no cloud sync.**

## Storage and retention

- Music analysis, fetched Oura data, and listening history are stored in a local database on your
  device.
- Because you are the only user and the sole controller of your own data, orbn keeps this data on
  your device for as long as you choose to keep it. There is no remote copy.
- **Deleting your data:** uninstalling orbn removes its local database and any cached data. You may
  also clear app data from your device settings.

## Your Oura data

orbn accesses the Oura API only with your explicit OAuth authorization, uses your Oura data solely
on your device to choose and visualize music, and never shares or sells it. You can **revoke orbn's
access at any time** from your Oura account settings. Your use of Oura data is also subject to
Oura's own terms and privacy policy.

## Children

orbn is not directed to children and does not knowingly process data from anyone under the age
required to hold an Oura account.

## Changes

This policy may change as the project evolves; the current version always lives in the
[project repository](https://github.com/earlyspark/orbn/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
